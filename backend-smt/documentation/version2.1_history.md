# SMT Manufacturing Execution System (MES)

## VERSION 2.1: SMT WORK ORDER HISTORY & DASHBOARD

- **Purpose of the page:** Centralized reporting dashboard where supervisors and managers can review historical progress of all SMT work orders.
- **What this page is used for:** Filtering and auditing past production runs, monitoring target vs actual output yields, and analyzing line balances.
- **What has changed compared to Version 2.0:** Minor updates to yield calculation logic to reflect the decommissioned SPI (Solder Paste Inspection) process step.
- **What new features have been added:** None.
- **What improvements have been made:** Process flow history and yield statistics calculations now reflect the direct logical transition from Stencil Printing to Pre AOI.
- **Any workflow changes:** None.
- **Any validation improvements:** None.
- **Any UI/UX improvements:** None.
- **Any search or filtering improvements:** None.
- **Any new business rules:** Yield statistics calculations bypass old SPI step parameters.
- **Benefits for operators:** Easy lookup of past run metrics.
- **Benefits for supervisors:** Accurate historical summaries and correct OEE calculations.
- **Benefits for production:** Seamless reporting of complete board histories.
- **Benefits for quality:** Reliable final inspection yield tracking.
- **Benefits for traceability:** Full historical records from printing through final packaging.
- **Overall outcome of the changes:** Consistent and accurate historical reporting aligned with the streamlined line layout.
